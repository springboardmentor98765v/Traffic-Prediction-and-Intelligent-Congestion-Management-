## Defect Status

- **Open** – Defect is identified and waiting for fixing.
- **In Progress** – Developer is currently fixing the defect.
- **Fixed** – Developer has completed the fix.
- **Retest** – QA needs to verify the fix.
- **Closed** – Defect is fixed and successfully verified.

## Severity

- **High** – Major functionality is affected.
- **Medium** – Functionality is partially affected.
- **Low** – Minor issue with limited impact.

## Priority

- **High** – Must be fixed immediately.
- **Medium** – Should be fixed before release.
- **Low** – Can be fixed later.
